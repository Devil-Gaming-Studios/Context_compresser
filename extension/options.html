settings

Backend

Point this at your Context Compressor backend. For any URL other than localhost/127.0.0.1, the browser will ask you to grant this extension permission to talk to that origin.

Defaults

Used by the popup, the floating in-page button, and the keyboard shortcut / right-click menu.

Enabled sites

Turn off the floating "compress" button on any site without disabling the extension entirely.

Shortcuts

Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K compresses the currently focused field. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+L opens this extension's popup. Right-click any editable field or selection for "Compress with Context Compressor." Manage or remap these at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
